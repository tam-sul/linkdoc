Why families join

The check-up finds it. Someone has to do something about it.

A school assessment tells you your child needs glasses, or has two teeth with decay, or is falling off their growth curve. Then the report ends and you are on your own — finding a clinic, taking a day off work, queueing, explaining the history again.

The same team, every time

You are assigned a nurse and a doctor who know your family. No queue, no starting the story again, no stranger reading a file for the first time.

They come to you

Scheduled home visits for check-ups, vitals, growth measurement and immunisation — without taking a day off work or sitting in a waiting room with a sick child.

One record that follows the child

Every measurement, every visit, every vaccine in one place. Next year's check-up is a comparison, not a fresh start.

The app

Everything about your child's health, in your pocket

Built for parents, not for doctors. Open it to see where your child sits on the growth curve, what vaccine is due next, and what the nurse wrote after the last visit.

Paying from abroad

You are not there. You can still be the one who arranges it.

Thousands of Ethiopians abroad send money home for a parent's medication or a child's hospital bill, and never really know what happened next.

With LinkDoc you pay from wherever you are and see exactly what was delivered: who visited, what they measured, what they found and what happens next. The care goes to your family in Addis. The report comes to your phone in Washington, Dubai or Frankfurt.
