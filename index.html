Why it matters

Some children are not struggling. They cannot see the board.

A child who cannot see, cannot hear, is in pain or is not sleeping does not learn — whatever the timetable says. Without screening, none of it is ever found, and the child is quietly marked down as a slow learner.

Vision and hearing

The two most common undetected barriers to learning, and the two most easily corrected. A pair of glasses can change a child’s entire school record.

Pain and sleep

Untreated dental pain and short sleep both destroy concentration. Neither shows up in a mark book, and neither gets mentioned by the child.

Chronic illness and absence

Uncontrolled asthma means repeated absence. The school sees an attendance problem. The cause is a health problem nobody has managed.

LinkDoc does not diagnose learning difficulties. It finds the health problems that look like them.

We screen every student, connect the findings to what teachers observe, and tell you where a health issue may be getting in the way of learning — so it can be checked properly. Never a conclusion about a child. Always a reason to look closer.

And then there is the day something goes wrong.

Most schools handle health as a set of problems nobody owns: a teacher improvises, the first-aid box is half empty, and no one has a record of which child is asthmatic — until an emergency reveals it.

What the parent receives

A real report, not a tick in a register

Every student is assessed across seven areas and more than seventy parameters. Within 72 hours their parent receives a digital report — colour-coded so they know instantly whether to do nothing, keep an eye, or see a clinician.

Findings flow into the student’s record, so next year’s assessment is a comparison, not a fresh start. Growth is tracked against WHO standards from the first visit.

Every finding gets a name and a date against it — who is doing something about it, and by when. We then follow it until it is closed. A screening that ends with a report is a leaflet; a screening that ends with a treated child is a health service.

Partnership

Your school earns from this too

When parents choose to buy health products through LinkDoc — family subscriptions, vaccination campaigns, vision or dental programmes — your school receives a share of collected revenue, paid quarterly.

It costs the school nothing and requires no work beyond letting us speak to parents. The share rises with your service level, from 10% on Assure to 15% on Complete and Partner.
